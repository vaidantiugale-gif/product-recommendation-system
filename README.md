# Retail AI Recommendation & Personalization Dataset

## Customer Behavior, Recommendation Signals & Personalized Shopping Analytics

Retail personalization is one of the most important applications of AI in modern ecommerce.

This dataset captures customer-product interactions, recommendation exposure, click behavior, cart actions, purchase outcomes, customer segments, product categories, pricing, discounts, and session-level shopping behavior.

It is designed for:

- recommendation systems
- personalization modeling
- click-through rate prediction
- conversion prediction
- customer segmentation
- ecommerce analytics
- retail AI dashboards
- machine learning experimentation

---

## Files Included

### retail_ai_recommendation_personalization_250k.csv

This file contains 250,000 retail AI recommendation and personalization interaction records.

Each row represents a customer-product recommendation interaction, including behavioral, recommendation, product, pricing, and purchase outcome signals.

## Dataset Highlights

## Column Descriptions

- customer_id: Unique customer identifier
- product_id: Unique product identifier
- customer_age: Customer age
- customer_segment: Behavioral customer segment
- loyalty_status: Customer loyalty tier
- country: Customer country
- device_type: Device used during shopping session
- traffic_source: Customer acquisition source
- product_category: Product category
- brand: Product brand
- product_price: Original product price
- discount_percentage: Discount applied
- final_price: Final product price after discount
- recommendation_type: Type of recommendation shown
- recommendation_position: Rank position of recommendation
- recommendation_score: AI recommendation relevance score
- clicked_recommendation: Whether recommendation was clicked
- added_to_cart: Whether product was added to cart
- purchased_after_recommendation: Whether product was purchased after recommendation
- quantity: Quantity purchased
- purchase_amount: Final purchase value
- session_duration_minutes: Session duration
- pages_viewed: Number of pages viewed
- search_queries: Number of searches during session
- past_views: Customer historical views
- past_purchases: Customer historical purchases
- avg_order_value: Customer average order value
- payment_method: Payment method
- time_of_day: Session time segment
- season: Shopping season
- interaction_date: Recommendation interaction date

## Why This Dataset Is Useful

Modern ecommerce platforms rely heavily on recommendation systems and personalization.

This dataset provides a practical retail AI environment where users can explore how recommendation scores, ranking positions, discounts, customer behavior, and product attributes influence clicks, cart additions, and purchases.

It can be used by beginners for EDA and by advanced users for recommendation modeling, ranking systems, and conversion prediction.
